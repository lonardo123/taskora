About Taskora

Our goal is to provide a clear and accessible platform for discovering online earning opportunities and tracking eligible rewards in one place.

Taskora is designed for users who want to explore online tasks and offers. Depending on availability, location, provider rules and account eligibility, users may be able to participate in activities and receive rewards credited to their accounts.

Taskora displays account-related information such as available balance and activity records through its application interface. Available activities and reward amounts may change over time.

Rewards and Payments

Taskora account balances and eligible reward transactions are managed according to the platform's applicable rules.

Reward eligibility

Rewards are not guaranteed merely by viewing an offer or opening an activity. The required action must be completed and accepted under the applicable task or offer provider rules.

Balance and withdrawal requests

Where withdrawal functionality is available, requests are subject to the platform's current minimum amounts, supported payment methods, account checks and any applicable review process.

Third-party providers

Some activities may be provided by external services. Their availability, tracking, eligibility requirements and processing times may be outside Taskora's direct control.

Please check the current rules shown inside the application before completing an activity or submitting a payment request. No specific earning amount or processing time is guaranteed.
